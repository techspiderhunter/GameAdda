**1. Information We Do Not Collect**

Game Adda is designed to provide instant, hassle-free gaming:

* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to play any games.

* **Game Data & Progress:** Your game scores, preferences, and local progress (e.g., Sudoku difficulty level or board state) are stored **locally on your device** via local storage/cache. This data is not uploaded to our external servers.

* Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
* Device model, operating system version, and screen resolution
* Approximate coarse location (country or city level derived from IP address)
* In-app ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)



These third parties process information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

---

**3. How We Use Information**

Any automatically processed technical data is used strictly to:

* Ensure games run smoothly without crashes or technical errors.
* Serve non-intrusive advertisements to support the free platform.
* Analyze overall app performance and stability.

**4. Data Sharing and Disclosure**

* We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to third parties.
